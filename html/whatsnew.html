What's New

This update adds new privacy and convenience options. They are all optional — nothing changes until you turn them on.

Auto-clear on lock
Optionally erase history, downloads and more whenever the profile locks — so a locked profile leaves no traces, even if the extension is removed.
Auto-lock on inactivity
Optionally lock the profile after a chosen period of no activity.
Language support
The interface now follows your browser language (English and Russian).
